Supply Chain Framework

The 4-Step Cocoa Value Chain

How True Harvest Cocoa manages every phase of production, quality assurance, and international fulfillment.

01

Ethical Farm Sourcing & Cooperative Support

We partner directly with registered smallholder farming cooperatives. We provide technical agronomy training, eco-friendly inputs, soil health monitoring, and guaranteed premium purchase prices above standard market rates.

Direct Purchasing Agroforestry Training Fair Trade Premiums
02

Precision Post-Harvest Processing & Quality Control

Harvested pods undergo centralized, monitored fermentation in wooden sweatboxes followed by sun-drying on raised bamboo tables. Moisture levels, bean cut tests, and flavor profiling are conducted at our regional processing hubs.

7-Day Controlled Fermentation Moisture & Cut Testing Grade-A Batch Selection
03

Digital Traceability & Warehouse Logistics

Each bag of cocoa is tagged with unique batch IDs linked to its cooperative origin, harvest date, and quality parameters. Beans are stored in climate-monitored warehouses to preserve aroma and prevent humidity degradation.

GPS Farm Mapping Jute Bag Barcoding Humidity Managed Storage
04

Global Export & Buyer Delivery

We manage all customs clearance, phytosanitary certifications, bill of lading documentation, and ocean freight logistics to ensure timely container shipments to chocolate processors and confectioners around the world.

Phytosanitary Certification Container Logistics FOB / CIF Contract Options
