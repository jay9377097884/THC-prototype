1. Project Overview & Objectives

This proposal outlines a custom corporate website to establish a clear, credible, and engaging online platform for True Harvest Cocoa. The website will be built leveraging HubSpot Content Hub Starter to present the company’s purpose, sustainable cocoa sourcing model, operational capabilities, and community impact to global buyers, agricultural partners, investors, and key stakeholders.

Core Business Objectives
  • Communicate True Harvest Cocoa's mission, ethical sourcing model, and commitment to farmer prosperity.
  • Provide a structured multi-page layout allowing prospective buyers to explore products, specifications, and sustainability credentials.
  • Deliver an accessible route for business inquiries through a professional enquiry form and direct WhatsApp chat integration.
  • Establish a responsive, high-performance visual identity built on HubSpot Content Hub Starter.

2. Detailed Project Scope & Deliverables

The scope encompasses full design, technical configuration, content implementation, testing, and deployment for approximately five primary pages, alongside essential integrations.

Deliverable Item Description & Key Elements Included
1. Home Page Executive hero banner, core value proposition, key operational highlights (Direct Farmer Partnerships, Quality Control, Traceable Logistics), corporate impact counters, and clear call-to-action sections.
2. About Us Page Company story ("From Bean to Business"), mission, vision, core values, philosophy, ethical commitments, and leadership/agronomy team profile grid.
3. Our Model Page Step-by-step visual supply chain framework (Ethical Sourcing → Post-Harvest Processing → Digital Traceability → Global Export), scalability breakdown, and EUDR compliance capabilities.
4. Impact & Resources Page Sustainability metrics dashboard (Farmers Empowered, Hectares Protected, Income Premiums), industry insights/updates, and downloadable PDF corporate report sections.
5. Contact Us Page Business contact details (Email, Phone, Headquarters address), interactive stakeholder enquiry form with subject categories and submission feedback, and instant WhatsApp connect bar.
6. Technical & Platform Setup HubSpot Content Hub Starter installation, DNS & domain mapping setup, SSL/HTTPS security configuration, and early deliverable temporary "Coming Soon" page during initial development.
7. Special Integrations Floating direct WhatsApp chat widget on all pages, custom browser favicon/identity setup, and mobile-responsive drawer menu.

3. Work Quality Standards & QA Process

The project adheres to strict technical and visual quality benchmarks to ensure a professional, secure, and performant deliverable:

🎨 Visual & Layout Quality
  • Professional typography, brand palette, and consistent header/footer alignment across all pages.
  • Fully responsive layout adapted for Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile screen viewports.
  • Custom responsive template development on HubSpot Content Hub Starter.
⚡ Performance & Compatibility
