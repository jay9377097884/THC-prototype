Who We Are

Our Story, Philosophy & Commitment

Built on a foundation of trust, ethical sourcing, and agricultural excellence, True Harvest Cocoa transforms how fine cocoa is cultivated, processed, and traded globally.

Company Story

Rooted in Passion for Cocoa & Farmer Prosperity

True Harvest Cocoa was established to address the critical gaps in the conventional cocoa supply chain. For generations, cocoa farmers faced volatile pricing, lack of technical support, and limited market access.

Our company was founded with a clear objective: build a modern, high-integrity corporate platform that directly connects cocoa farming communities with world-class chocolate producers. By implementing rigorous quality benchmarks and transparent trading terms, we create lasting value for every stakeholder in the chain.
